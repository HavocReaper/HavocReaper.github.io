01 / About me

Building safer systems, one flaw at a time.

I'm a hands-on Application Security Consultant with around three years of experience in manual web application penetration testing, network VAPT and mobile security assessments.

My work focuses on identifying complex business logic flaws, authentication bypasses, IDOR, SSRF and chained vulnerabilities that automated scanners typically miss. I'm proficient with Burp Suite, Kali Linux, Nmap and Python for custom PoCs and testing automation.

I work closely with development and infrastructure teams to translate technical findings into clear impact, reproducible proof-of-concepts and pragmatic remediation roadmaps — because finding the bug is only half the job.
